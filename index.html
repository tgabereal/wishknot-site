About the game

An endless wishing well

Wishknot is a physics-driven action roguelite set inside an endless wishing well. Swing through caverns, turn enemies into anchors, and redirect your momentum with thrust. Tow an anvil into a fight, snare a hostile creature, or release at just the right moment to launch yourself past danger.

Enter a world of chaos

The well changes with every descent. Explore strange procedural biomes, break open new routes, and face bosses hidden in the depths. Collect stars, combine enchantments, and adapt to curses that change how your run unfolds.

Scattered through the dark are secrets, wishes, and lost characters waiting to be found. Reach them, tow them home, and unlock new abilities for future descents.

On your terms

Build speed through clean arcs, preserve it with precise releases, and spend it on a devastating impact. Anything caught on your rope can become a weapon, an obstacle, or your next anchor.

Slip past enemies quietly or make an entrance that brings the cavern down on you. Noise draws attention, and enemies can merge into larger threats. Every encounter gives you another reason to improvise.

Features

What the rope can do

  • One physical rope

    Swing, anchor, tow, snare, sling, and crush.

  • Terrain you can reshape

    Open routes and turn the environment into part of your attack.

  • Momentum you can master

    Physics-driven encounters shaped by mass, tension, collisions, and terrain.

  • Builds that change your approach

    Combine enchantments across rope, thrust, health, and time.

  • Stars and curses

    Discover new powers and adapt to dangerous changes.

  • Slow motion and rewind

    Give yourself time to react or rethink your approach.

  • Playable entirely with a mouse

    Precision movement with your controls close at hand.

  • Built-in replays

    Export MP4s and GIFs of your best escapes and most spectacular disasters.
